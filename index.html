Clear Aligners for a More Discreet Option

Some patients prefer an orthodontic treatment that is less noticeable than traditional braces. Clear aligners may provide a discreet alternative for eligible patients.

Clear aligners are removable trays designed to gradually guide teeth toward planned positions. Because every orthodontic case is different, an orthodontist should first evaluate the patient's teeth and bite before determining whether clear aligners are appropriate.

SmileCrew offers clear aligner treatment alongside conventional braces, giving eligible patients an opportunity to discuss different approaches during their consultation.
